# Research Coverage Matrix

This defines the public sample's coverage.

The coverage focuses on self-serve consumer products and audience businesses that one person could test. B2B products that require a sales pipeline are kept out of the primary shortlist, though their presence is recorded when it affects a niche's likely economics.

| Opportunity area | First-pass search coverage | Snapshot status | Why include it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Shopping deals and coupons | `coupon deals`, `discount alert bot`, `food deals`, `restaurant deals`, `grocery deals` | Sampled | Affiliate conversion is directly observable; consumer utility is easy to explain. |
| Cashback and price alerts | `SaaS deals`, `software discounts`, `cashback deals`, `price tracker bot`, `shopping assistant bot` | Sampled | Could support affiliate revenue; consumer purchase intent needs validation. |
| Jobs and career discovery | `job alerts`, `remote jobs`, `remote developer jobs`, `freelance jobs`, `micro jobs`, `remote internships`, `job application bot`, Chinese job terms | Sampled | Clear recurring need; many visible models rely on employer placement or recruiting sales. |
| Scholarships and financial aid | `scholarship alerts`, `scholarship bot`, Chinese scholarship terms | Sampled | High-value information need; paid consumer products and repeat use need validation. |
| Language learning and exams | `English learning bot`, `IELTS bot`, `language exchange`, `language learning bot`, `exam prep bot`, `online course deals` | Sampled | Habit-driven use can support premium tools or learning products. |
| AI and software discovery | `AI tools bot`, `AI for designers`, `AI productivity tools`, `SaaS deals`, `software discounts` | Sampled | Fast-changing demand and potential affiliate routes; high competition risk. |
| Travel and transport deals | `flight deals`, `travel alert bot`, `travel itinerary bot`, `hotel deals`, `train deals` | Sampled | Purchase intent can be monetized through referrals; seasonality matters. |
| Expat life, relocation, and visas | `expat guide`, `visa updates`, `apartment rental`, `housing expats`, `digital nomad community` | Sampled | Strong practical need; accuracy and geography-specific upkeep are costly. |

| Productivity and personal organization | `productivity bot`, `study planner bot` | Sampled | Strong bot fit; crowded market and retention are open questions. |
| Books, media, and entertainment discovery | `book summaries`, `reading club` | Sampled | Good channel fit; rights and monetization need separate assessment. |
| Personal safety and emergency assistance | `personal safety bot` | Sampled | Useful consumer utility; trust, reliability, and false-positive handling are critical. |

## Coverage notes

For each area, the sample uses at least two intent phrases where practical, includes channel and bot results where the platform returns them, records the search date and language, and inspects public metrics for a small sample. A row is not considered validated just because a keyword returned results. All declared areas have a documented sample or a documented search failure; result depth and language coverage remain uneven, and Telegram has not been exhaustively indexed.
